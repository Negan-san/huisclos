Advice note, 15 September 2026

Ms [[Ivara Wensholt|PERSON]] asks whether a rehearsal venue may retain her deposit after cancelling an evening booking. She lives at [[48 Lantern Quay, Bristol, BS1 6QZ, United Kingdom|ADDRESS]] and was born on [[21 April 1989|BIRTH_DATE]]. The booking was made with [[Kestrel Loom Studios Ltd|ORGANIZATION]]. The private matter reference is [[KL-ADVICE-26-731|CASE_NUMBER]].

The written terms distinguish cancellation by the customer from closure of the building. The email announcing closure refers to an electrical inspection, but does not say that the customer caused the problem. Before demanding a particular remedy, we should obtain the complete terms supplied at the time of payment and the notice explaining the closure. A later website version may contain different conditions.

The venue coordinator, Mr [[Tavren Bellwick|PERSON]], offered a weekday replacement. That proposal may be useful, but the original booking was for a group travelling at the weekend. We should ask whether the offer is optional and whether declining it would affect the deposit. [[Bellwick|PERSON]] has not yet answered that question in writing.

The client can be reached at [[ivara.wensholt@example.com|EMAIL]] or [[+44 7700 900421|PHONE]]. The venue uses [[bookings.kestrelloom@example.org|EMAIL]]. Her membership code is [[MEM-KL-572|CUSTOM]]; it should appear only where needed to locate the account. It is not a substitute for the matter reference.

The next letter should set out the booking date, the closure notice and the requested response date. It should attach the payment receipt without forwarding unrelated group messages. We should keep a record of any replacement arrangements and actual additional costs. A speculative estimate of future inconvenience should not be presented as an expense already incurred. If the venue provides a reasoned refusal, the client can then decide whether a negotiated credit meets her practical needs.
