Draft return protocol, circulated for discussion on 26 October 2026

Background

[[Alderwake Systems LLC|ORGANIZATION]] and [[Nacre Vale Assembly Ltd|ORGANIZATION]] propose to end a temporary arrangement for testing prototype equipment. This protocol is intended to organise inspection, collection and the recording of reservations. It does not become binding until authorised representatives have approved the schedules and signed the final version. The private matter reference is [[PROTO-AV-26-876|CASE_NUMBER]]. No provision should be read as resolving an unrelated dispute merely because it concerns the same equipment.

The owner's representative is Ms [[Lysanne Corvenholt|PERSON]], with correspondence address [[219 Wren Compass Boulevard, Suite 8, Oakmere, WA 98174, USA|ADDRESS]]. The testing company's representative is Mr [[Osvan Pellwick|PERSON]], at [[31 Willow Glass Mews, Bath, BA2 6ZR, United Kingdom|ADDRESS]]. Each will confirm the authority under which they sign. A person arranging access to the loading area may have logistical responsibilities without authority to accept a financial settlement.

1. Equipment within scope

The first schedule will identify the test units, detachable parts, packaging and documents to be returned. It will distinguish items supplied by the owner from consumables purchased during testing. Any disputed item will be placed on a separate list rather than silently omitted from the inventory. The parties will record the reason for the dispute and agree who will hold the item while the issue is considered. Neither party may dispose of it simply because collection of the undisputed units has taken place.

The internal asset reference [[ASSET-AW-458|CUSTOM]] identifies the principal test frame. It is not a court reference or a personal identification number. Any photographs should be linked to the schedule entry they illustrate. A general photograph of a room will not establish the condition of a concealed connector or show whether a removable part was present inside a closed case.

2. Inspection arrangements

The provisional inspection date is 12 November 2026. The parties should allow enough time to compare the equipment with the schedule and record any failure to operate. They will agree in advance which power supply and test conditions are safe. The protocol does not require an operator to conduct a demonstration that would damage a unit or expose participants to an avoidable hazard. If a test cannot be completed, the reason and the proposed follow-up should be recorded.

The inspection record will distinguish observed facts from explanations offered by either party. It may state that a connector is loose without attributing the cause immediately. Each representative may add a concise reservation. Signing the record confirms that the observations were recorded, subject to those reservations; it should not automatically release every claim about defects that could not reasonably be seen during the inspection.

4. Communications

Notices concerning this protocol should be sent to [[returns.alderwake@example.com|EMAIL]] and [[operations.nacrevale@example.org|EMAIL]]. The telephone contacts for the collection day are [[+1 206 555 0186|PHONE]] and [[+44 7700 900864|PHONE]]. A call may address an immediate access problem, but a lasting change to scope, destination or payment requires written confirmation. The carrier should receive the practical delivery information it needs, rather than an unrestricted copy of the parties' legal correspondence.

[[Corvenholt|PERSON]] may nominate an alternative recipient in writing. [[Pellwick|PERSON]] will identify the person who actually releases the equipment at the loading point. A badge or access code is not sufficient evidence that someone can agree to a waiver. If the nominated person changes on the day, the collection team should verify the instruction through a contact already known to both parties before proceeding.

5. Costs and unresolved matters

The parties will separately list agreed transport charges, disputed testing costs and any proposed credit. An unsigned spreadsheet should not be treated as a final account merely because its total appears in a delivery email. Supporting invoices should identify the work and period concerned. A reservation about costs may remain open when the equipment is collected, provided the receipt makes that position clear. The parties should avoid wording that turns physical handover into an unintended full settlement.

6. Records and final approval

Each party may retain the records reasonably needed to establish what was delivered and what remained disputed. Personal contact information should not be copied into publicity material about the prototype. Technical drawings and confidential operating notes will be listed separately so that a missing attachment can be identified before signature. The protocol does not grant either party a right to publish the other's designs or imply that possession of a drawing establishes ownership of every underlying right.

Before approval, the representatives will review the inventory, transport instructions and outstanding reservations together. Any incomplete schedule should be marked as incomplete and resolved expressly. The final document will state its effective date and the number of signed counterparts. Until then, this working draft records proposals only, and the parties' existing arrangements must be considered on their own terms.
