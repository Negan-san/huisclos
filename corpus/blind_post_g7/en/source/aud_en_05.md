Adviser's note

The inspection should produce a short record of what was demonstrated and what remains uncertain. The customer should not be asked to sign a general release merely to collect the item. If the vibration persists, the parties can discuss another test and the evidence needed to identify its cause. Any claim for additional costs should distinguish actual expenditure from a quotation for work that has not yet been ordered.
