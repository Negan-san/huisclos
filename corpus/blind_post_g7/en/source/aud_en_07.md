3. What the correspondence establishes

A message dated 6 September 2026 says that the records remain safely stored. A later message refers to closing the project account. Neither statement identifies which files exist, their formats or the retention period proposed. We should request a dated inventory and confirmation that no deletion or alteration will occur while the handover is being arranged. The request should avoid asserting that files have already been destroyed, because the available evidence does not establish that.

The client has a partial export made by Ms [[Tavelle Orinshaw|PERSON]], a production assistant. That export contains draft schedules and several signed pages, but no reliable index. [[Orinshaw|PERSON]] can explain how it was obtained and whether any folder was deliberately excluded. Her evidence should be recorded in her own terms. We should not ask her to reconstruct a complete history from memory or treat the export as proof that every current file is preserved.

4. Proposed handover process

Stage | Required record | Decision after review
Preservation | Dated confirmation of retained material | Set a workable handover date
Inventory | File categories, formats and physical media | Identify omissions or disputed items
Inspection | Agreed opening checks and transfer log | Accept delivery with specific reservations
Closure | Receipt distinguishing delivery from settlement | Reconcile the remaining account

The inspection should establish that the supplied files can be opened using the agreed software, without requiring the client to install an unknown tool. A technical checksum may help verify that a transfer is unchanged, but it does not establish the legal completeness of the records. Any failed opening check should be described precisely, with enough information for the provider to reproduce it. A general statement that the archive is unusable would be less useful than a clear list of affected items.

5. Contacts and authority

[[Halverden|PERSON]] will coordinate instructions through [[eirwen.halverden@example.com|EMAIL]] and [[+44 7700 900752|PHONE]]. Her internal project identifier is [[RM-ARCH-639|CUSTOM]]. It locates the project but does not authorise a courier or another employee to receive confidential files. Any substitute recipient should be nominated in writing, with the scope of that authority stated. The delivery receipt should identify who actually attended and what they received.

The provider has suggested that [[Densmere|PERSON]] no longer handles daily administration. We should ask for one authorised contact rather than copying the entire staff list into the dispute. The first letter can describe the categories of documents required without attaching the participant records themselves. If a sample is needed to explain a missing category, it should contain only the information necessary for that purpose. Public references to the Civil Code and ordinary project dates are context, not instructions to disclose unrelated personal material.

6. Financial position and next steps

The earlier payment instruction used IBAN [[{{IBAN#1}}|IBAN]]. That detail belongs in the account reconciliation and should not be treated as a fresh payment instruction. We should compare the original scope, the request for extra work and the invoice breakdown before suggesting a final balance. A practical agreement to collect the records need not settle every question about the provider's remuneration.

The proposed first step is a focused preservation and inventory request, allowing ten days for a substantive response. If the records are available, a controlled handover may resolve the urgent practical problem. If a specific loss is established, the client should preserve evidence of reconstruction costs and interrupted tasks. Estimated future inconvenience should remain separate from expenditure already incurred. Further advice will depend on the complete agreement, the inventory and the provider's response; no court application has been authorised by this note.
