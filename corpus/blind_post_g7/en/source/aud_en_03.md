Draft custody agreement, 2 October 2026

This draft records a proposed arrangement between [[Hollow Reed Exhibits LLC|ORGANIZATION]] and Ms [[Elwen Strayford|PERSON]]. It concerns six display panels and their detachable supports. The custodian will hold them without acquiring ownership. No signature is requested until the condition schedule has been completed and both parties have confirmed the collection arrangements.

1. Delivery will take place at [[326 Paper Lantern Avenue, Suite 14, Bellspire, CA 94726, USA|ADDRESS]]. Each package must be labelled against the schedule before unloading. Any visible damage should be recorded while the delivery representative is still present. A signature acknowledging the number of boxes will not by itself confirm that their contents are undamaged.

2. Mr [[Cevrin Mossvale|PERSON]] will coordinate access for the company. His contact details are [[cevrin.mossvale@example.org|EMAIL]] and [[+1 (415) 555-0142 ext. 7|PHONE]]. The customer's collection code is [[PANEL-HR-694|CUSTOM]]. That code locates the stored items; it does not authorise an unknown bearer to remove them.

3. [[Strayford|PERSON]], whose date of birth is [[9 December 1991|BIRTH_DATE]], will nominate any alternative collector in writing. Her correspondence address is [[81 Alder Compass Road, Unit 6, Windmere, OR 97382, USA|ADDRESS]]. She can receive notices at [[elwen.strayford@example.com|EMAIL]]. The private file reference is [[CUST-HR-26-318|CASE_NUMBER]].

4. The proposed collection date is 18 November 2026. If access becomes impossible, the custodian must give a reason and offer another practical date. The parties will separately agree any documented additional storage charge. Silence in response to a revised date will not amount to consent to dispose of the panels.

5. The final schedule should identify which packaging may be discarded and which must be returned. The parties should also record the procedure for checking a concealed defect. This draft leaves the insurance limit for discussion rather than assuming that a general business policy covers the customer's property.
